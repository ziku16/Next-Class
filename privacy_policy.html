Privacy Policy

NextClass is an offline-first timetable manager. We collect zero personal data, require no accounts, and store your schedules exclusively on your device.

1. Overview & Commitment

NextClass ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") was created to simplify university and college course scheduling without compromising user privacy. We firmly believe that your academic schedule belongs to you alone.

NextClass does not track you, does not require an account, and operates entirely locally on your device.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All data entered into NextClass is kept locally in an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite database on your device:

  • Course & Subject Names: The names and codes of your enrolled lectures and courses.
  • Timetable Schedules: Days of the week, class start times, and end times.
  • Classrooms & Halls: Physical lecture halls, laboratories, or seminar rooms.
  • Instructor Details: Instructor or professor names that you add.
  • Quizzes & Deadlines: Quiz titles, subjects, dates, and times.
  • Reminder Settings: Your configured notification offsets (e.g. 10 minutes before class) and daily digest preferences.

This information is stored strictly in your device's private application storage and is never uploaded to our servers.

3. No Accounts or Personal Profiles

NextClass requires no registration or authentication. We never collect or store:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Student ID or university enrollment credentials
  • Location data or GPS telemetry
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs

4. 100% On-Device Timetable Scanning

NextClass offers a timetable scanning feature allowing you to photograph or upload a schedule image for automated text extraction:

  • All image preprocessing, optical character recognition (OCR), and schedule parsing run 100% locally and offline on your device.
  • Your schedule photos and timetable details are NEVER uploaded to any cloud server, third-party AI model, or external service.
  • NextClass does not transmit, retain, archive, view, or sell your schedule images. All data stays strictly on your device.

5. Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

Permission System Identifier Reason & Usage
Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers class start reminders and quiz alerts to your lock screen and notification shade (Android 13+).
Exact Alarms SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
USE_EXACT_ALARM		 Ensures notifications trigger at the exact scheduled minute, even during Android battery-saver Doze mode.
Reboot Restoration RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Automatically restores and reschedules your weekly alarms after your device is restarted.
Camera / Gallery CAMERA / Media Picker Requested dynamically only if you choose to photograph or import a timetable image.

6. Zero Advertising & Zero Analytics

  • No Advertising SDKs: NextClass does not display ads and contains no commercial ad-network code.
  • No Analytics SDKs: NextClass contains no behavioral analytics, trackers, or telemetric logging libraries.
  • No Data Monetization: We never sell, rent, trade, or share your schedule or personal information.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full autonomy over your data:

  • You can modify or delete individual classes, timetables, and quizzes at any time in the app.
  • You can wipe all data at any time via Android Settings > Apps > NextClass > Storage > Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling NextClass immediately and permanently erases the SQLite database, preferences, and all scheduled notifications.

8. Children's Privacy

NextClass is suitable for students of all ages. Because NextClass does not collect any personally identifiable information from any user, no personal data from children under 13 is collected.
