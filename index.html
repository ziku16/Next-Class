FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got questions? We've got answers.

How does the AI timetable scanning work? +
When you snap or upload a photo of your schedule, NextClass natively compresses it and sends it securely to Google Gemini 3.8 Flash Vision API. Gemini analyzes the grid structure, detects lecture subjects, start and end times, and room locations, outputting clean structured data for you to verify.
Is my schedule data stored on external servers? +
No. Aside from the transient photo scan request processed by Gemini Vision, 100% of your timetable classes, quiz dates, and personal settings are stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database. We do not track your location, and there is no user tracking.
Will notifications still ring if my phone restarts or enters battery saver mode? +
Yes! NextClass implements Android's Exact Alarm APIs (`exactAllowWhileIdle`) and an automatic `BOOT_COMPLETED` broadcast receiver. When your device restarts, NextClass automatically calculates and restores all weekly lecture and quiz alarms in the background.
Can I edit or add classes manually without scanning? +
Yes! You have full control. You can edit any class, add new slots manually, change room numbers, assign different color tags, or adjust start and end times at any point from the Review or Week screen.
Is NextClass completely free? Are there in-app purchases or ads? +
NextClass is 100% free with zero ads, zero subscriptions, and zero paywalls. It was created by students, for students, to eliminate academic chaos.
